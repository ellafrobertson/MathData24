## **About the Project**

The **Dolphin vs Shark Classifier** is an AI-powered tool developed to assist marine conservationists, ecotourism businesses, and educators. It uses advanced convolutional neural networks (AlexNet) to classify marine species with high accuracy.

### **Key Features**
- Custom-trained AlexNet model.
- Dataset of dolphin and shark images.
- 85% validation accuracy.
- Applications in marine research and conservation.

### **Mission Statement**
To harness the power of AI to classify marine species, support conservation efforts, and educate people worldwide about the importance of protecting our oceans.
